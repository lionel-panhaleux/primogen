## Question

What kind of meta should I expect for the upcoming GP in Vienna?

## Answer given

I can't give you Vienna's field specifically. Primogen only serves the decks that **won** events (TWDA). It has no full-field standings and no breakdown by region or event, so this is the general winning meta and not a forecast for one GP. The newest winning deck is dated 2026-10-25.

**The last 12 months** (2025-10-26 to 2026-10-25): 358 winning decks, from events totalling 7,609 players. Each share is weighted by event size.

| Archetype | 12 months | Last 6 months | Trend over the last 6 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gangrel Resistance | 11.1% | 12.9% | +4.2, clearly the top deck |
| Pop Captivation | 6.1% | 4.1% | −4.6, came in fast and is now cooling |
| The Unnamed | 5.0% | 7.0% | +4.6 |
| Lasombra Pop | 4.5% | 5.7% | +2.7, but from only 2 decks in 6 months |
| Ravnos Bonds | 4.5% | 4.8% | stable |
| Living Museum | 3.5% | 3.0% | −1.4 |

Decks that fit no archetype account for **10.7%** over 12 months and **13.8%** over the last 6. A meaningful slice of winners are off-archetype.

Falling over the last 6 months: Haqim Royalty (4.6% → 0.9%), Juliet's Dream (4.6% → 1.3%) and Protean Barons (3.8% → 0.6%).

**Cards behind the move in the last 6 months**, measured as the share of players whose event a deck with the card won:
- Rising: Anarch Convert (+10.2), Elder Impersonation (+9.8), Lost in Crowds (+9.6), The Coven (+8.7), Secure Haven (+8.5) and Enkil Cog (+7.7). These are mostly the stealth/bleed engines of Gangrel Resistance, The Unnamed and Ravnos Bonds.
- Falling: Eyes of Argus (−10.6), Telepathic Misdirection (−9.9), Papillon (−8.3) and Govern the Unaligned (−8.0).

**What to prepare for**, based on the numbers above: stealth bleed is the top threat (the rising stealth/bleed cards and the share of the top stealth-bleed archetypes). Vote decks (Pop Captivation and Lasombra Pop, both built on Banishment and Deflection) remain the second axis. How those two pressures will play out at a single GP is my impression, not data.
