## Question

If I have combat crypt with ANI is there any reason to take Trap over Conquer the Beast?

## Answer given

**Yes, three reasons favour Trap even with Animalism in the crypt. Conquer the Beast wins only for a vampire with superior ANI who wants a way out.**

The two cards share their timing and engine: 3 counters, reset each time a card is played, one press each round. They differ in three ways.

**1. Equipment.** Conquer the Beast says *"This vampire cannot use equipment this combat."* Trap has no such clause. If your combat plan includes weapons, Trap is the only one of the two that works with them.

**2. Who owns the press.** The sitting rules director (L. Scott Johnson, LSJ) drew the distinction: *"Compare: 'combat has a press' (Trap) to 'You get one press' (Conquer the Beast). In the former, the combat itself has a press, in the latter, the combatant has a press."* ([LSJ 1998](https://usenet.krcg.org/t/6iew9bqYFVY/#m5)). In practice:
- **Effects that stop your vampire from pressing beat Conquer.** LSJ confirmed that inferior Terror Frenzy (*"the opposing minion cannot … press to continue combat"*) stops Conquer the Beast's press and combat ends ([LSJ 2004](https://usenet.krcg.org/t/p-Xf1uFNdPE/#m40)).
- **Trap ignores them.** A minion that cannot press can still play Trap, and the press still happens, because *"the minion is not the one pressing"* ([LSJ 20040521](https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=102012#r-5HO47547)). Trap's press is also applied before any other press ([ANK 20200525-1](https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=102012#r-OUHXMFLT)).

**3. Who can play it.** Trap has no requirement, so your inferior-ANI vampires and your allies can use it too. At inferior, Conquer the Beast's presses are mandatory. Trap's presses are automatic as well, so at inferior Conquer gives you nothing over Trap and costs you your equipment.

**Where Conquer the Beast wins:** at superior ANI its presses are optional (*"[ANI] As above, but the presses are optional"*). Trap's presses keep coming until 3 rounds pass with no card played, and only the opponent can choose to press to end. With superior Conquer you can stop pressing when the combat turns against you.

**Play rate:** Trap is in 36 tournament-winning decks since 2021 and Conquer the Beast in 5. Most Trap decks are not Animalism decks, though (Nephandus, Piper War Ghoul, Hecata), so the count says little about your slot. In decks near the ones that play Conquer the Beast, the cards that win the slot are Carrion Crows, Pack Alpha and Bear's Skin, not Trap.

**Bottom line:**
- Pick **Trap** if you use equipment, if many of your vampires have inferior ANI, or if your table plays cards that deny presses.
- Pick **Conquer the Beast** if your fighters have superior ANI and you value the option to stop pressing.
