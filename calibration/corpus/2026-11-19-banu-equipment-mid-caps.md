## Question

https://vdb.im/decks/7dcfd9fcb

Theory: Standard-ish Banu (lots of Haqim's Law, Hunger + Blur/Lightning Reflexes) is reliant on assembling large multi-card combos in hand, and over-reliance on Priority Contract means if you fail to assemble a kill you get stuck unable to clear your previous (unique) Priority Contract and lose momentum. Instead: Run more BH mid-caps (especially Kalinda, but also the cheap princes), and spend some of the pool saved on influencing high-caps on equipment. I'm wondering if anyone could offer final suggestions or things I might've overlooked. I mostly have V5 cards, and don't have access to concealed/disguised weapons or zip guns

## Answer given

Nice list. The plan of fewer high-caps and a gun toolbox is sound. Winning Haqim Royalty decks already do a version of it: .44 Magnum shows up in 18 of the 41 winners since 2021, Magic of the Smith in 15. A few things in the list work against that plan, though.

**1. Kalinda is a primogen, not a prince. That matters more than it looks.**
Second Tradition: Domain, Fourth Tradition, Anathema and Archon all say *"Requires a prince or justicar."* Kalinda ×3 and Khadija are primogen, so 4 of your 12 vampires can't use any of your 8 Second Traditions. Leaning further into Kalinda makes your defence worse.
Her disciplines are also *tha* CEL OBF, with inferior Thaumaturgy. On her:
- Hunger of Marduk steals 1, not 2.
- Hunter's Mark doesn't get the superior *"the opposing minion cannot strike"* mode, which needs both CEL and THA superior.
- Magic of the Smith is +1 stealth instead of +3.
- Rego Motum prevents 2 instead of 4.

She's good at stealth bleeding, but she's the worst vampire in the crypt at the combat you're building around. Winners run her as a 1-of (14 of 41 decks, always 1 copy). If you want cheap mid-caps, cheap *princes* do the same job and keep Second Trad live: Warmaksan (5) and Kassandra (6), both superior THA. I'd go to 1–2 Kalinda and use the slots on a 3rd Warmaksan or Kassandra.

**2. Your "two-card combat" needs more additional strikes, and Taste of Vitae doesn't fit it.**
- Hunger's strike can be reused within the round: *"The provided strike can be chosen and used more than once during the round."* ([ANK 2022](https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=102227#r-TJMVKX6Y)). Every additional strike is another steal 2. Your extra strikes are 4 Side Strike + 3 Pursuit (both only at superior CEL) + 1 Quickness, with no Blur. 38 of the 41 winners play Blur, about 4 copies on average. If you don't own Blur, Lightning Reflexes does the same job (at CEL: *"Burn X blood to get X additional strikes"*).
- **Taste of Vitae doesn't work with Hunger.** It gains *"blood equal to the amount of blood lost by the opposing vampire to damage"*, and stealing is not damage (rulebook, [Steal Blood](https://archon.vekn.net/help/rules#steal-blood)). Card history, RTR 1996: Taste *"will not … give blood for … stolen blood."* With 3 guns and 2 Blood Fury, 3 Taste will often be dead. Only 2 of 41 winners play it.
- Rego Motum: you have 2. 36 of 41 winners play it, about 3 copies.

**3. Hunger empties a vampire, but a gun is what puts it in torpor.** This is your best argument for guns.
Steal *"does not count as damage"*, and a vampire only goes to torpor when it can't mend damage ([Damage Resolution](https://archon.vekn.net/help/rules#5-damage-resolution)). An all-Hunger combat leaves the target at 0 blood but still ready.
The two-card trade you want is: superior Hunter's Mark (they can't strike back) + Hunger, or Hunger + an additional strike with the .44's 2R. Steal first, then damage they can't mend. The Magnum's maneuver also gets you to long range, which Hunger's ranged strike ignores.

**4. Priority Contract: one copy is already the low end.**
Your worry about getting stuck is real only when the kill fails. The card clears itself on success: *"If the attached minion is about to leave the ready region, you can burn this card to gain 3 pool"*, and that includes the controller being ousted ([ANK 2018](https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=101487#r-WHWOEBQD)). Superior Hunter's Mark (you have 4) is the non-unique way to start a fight. 40 of 41 winners play Priority Contract, about 3 copies. A 2nd copy is cheap insurance.

**5. Ashur Tablets ×6** is a normal count (winners run 6 or 9). Remember it's one per turn and needs three in play.

**6. On your draws.** 0 of 8 Second Traditions in 50 cards out of 85 happens about 0.05% of the time (1 in 2000). That was a freak shuffle, not a sign the count is wrong. With 8 copies, about half your opening hands hold one.

**Suggested swap (no concealed weapons or Zip Guns needed):**
- −3 Taste of Vitae → +2 Blur (or Lightning Reflexes), +1 Rego Motum
- −1 Kalinda → +1 Warmaksan or Kassandra Tassaki
- −1 Camera Phone → +1 Kevlar Vest (a Magic of the Smith target; 9 of the 12 winners with a Vest also run the .44)
- Optional: +1 Priority Contract for your weakest card (Narrow Minds or Conceal)

Good luck at the tournament!
