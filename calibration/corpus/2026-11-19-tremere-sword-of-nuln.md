## Question

Like not competetively ofcourse, but maybe a workable concept for causual play. I was thinking
about it with Eulogio for the path of Death.
Since you could only really just block to get into combat effeciently or use the few guys with

Now with Path of death I think there could be some uses of oblivion cards for rushing and
maneuvering and maybe use Morbidity since I feel sad about the card not seeing much use.
Any thoughts on the concept?

## Answer given

It's a workable casual concept, and Morbidity is actually the card that makes the Sword good. Read together, the two texts combine, and that combination is the deck.

**The engine: Sword of Nuln + Morbidity**

- **Sword of Nuln** ([card](https://static.krcg.org/card/swordofnuln.jpg)), Tremere, unique, 1 pool: "Strike: strength damage. Vampires must spend twice the normal amount of blood to heal the damage from this weapon's strikes." If the target can't pay, "he pays what he can, still fails to heal the damage and goes to torpor empty" ([LSJ 20090901](https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=101920#r-5UKXD3MU)).
- **Morbidity** ([card](https://static.krcg.org/card/morbidity.jpg)), obl or tha, Path of Death and the Soul: "Put this card in play and move up to 2 blood from the opposing vampire to this card." At superior, "combat cards cost the opposing vampire +1 blood". The blood comes back only "After combat ends".
- Together: Morbidity strips 2 blood before range, then each point of Sword damage costs them 2 blood to mend. Example: a 4-blood vampire drops to 2, takes 1 strength damage, and needs 2 blood to mend it, so it's empty. A second point or a second round puts it in torpor. The blood on Morbidity doesn't return until combat ends, and by then they've already failed to mend ([rulebook, Damage Resolution](https://archon.vekn.net/help/rules#5-damage-resolution)). Superior Morbidity also taxes their combat cards, so they have even less blood to answer with.

**Eulogio fits**

[Eulogio Sánchez de los Reyes](https://static.krcg.org/card/eulogiosanchezdelosreyesg6.jpg) is a G6 Tremere (so Sword of Nuln is his) with AUS DOM OBL THA at superior, and he follows Path of Death and the Soul. That matters: "A card printing a Path icon requires a vampire following that Path" ([advanced rules, Other traits](https://vtes-biased.github.io/vtes-advanced-rules/#576-other-traits)). So Morbidity needs a Death and the Soul vampire, and he plays it at superior from either discipline. His own ability ("look at and reorder the top 5 cards of your library, and unlock as a +1 stealth action that costs 1 blood") digs for the Sword and for Morbidity.

- **Getting the Sword:** Magic of the Smith (tha) searches your library for an equipment card and equips it, as a +3 stealth action at superior. Eulogio has superior THA, so the Sword arrives reliably.
- **Path of Death and the Soul** (the master) gives "1 blood from the blood bank" plus a Master: Discipline card each time another Methuselah's minion is burned. Torpor alone doesn't burn anything, so it pays off only once you diablerize or burn what the Sword put down. It's a nice bonus. Don't count on it.

**The friction you named, and a bit more**

- **Getting into combat.** Hunter's Mark is [cel][tha], and Eulogio has no Celerity, so it only works on other crypt members who have it. Without a combat-entry action you're mostly blocking, which is fine for a toolbox wall: superior Auspex gives the deck its intercept. Look at your Dominate and Obtenebration cards for ways to start a fight.
- **One strike per round.** The Sword competes with Theft of Vitae for your strike each round, so choose per round. Theft is ranged and refuels you. The Sword is the finisher once Morbidity has drained them.
- **Unique.** Only one Sword is in play at a time. Eulogio, or whoever wields it, is the only one who hits. Another minion can take it over later ([ANK 20180719-3](https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=101920#r-G2IUHZW6)).
- **Crypt grouping.** Eulogio is group 6, so the rest of the crypt has to come from groups 5–6 or 6–7, with other Path of Death and the Soul Sabbat vampires to share the Morbidity.

**Track record:** no tournament-winning deck since 2021-07 plays Sword of Nuln, so there's no winning list to compare against. That's no verdict on a casual build. It just means you're off the beaten track.

If you sketch a list, I can run it through the deck tool to check the crypt groups, who meets which card, and the costs.

## Correction

1. **Name collision.** The master card Path of Death and the Soul is a legacy card with its own
   purpose; it has nothing to do with the Sabbat Path Eulogio follows. Listing it under "Eulogio
   fits" tied the two together.
2. **Too agreeing: the level went ungraded.** The concept is a weak module, fun but not
   competitive, and the answer should say so. No tournament winner plays the Sword; the answer
   read that as "no verdict".
3. **The engine misidentified.** The plan's real requirement is an answer to Strike: combat
   ends, the most prominent combat defense; with it, the Sword is a brutal engine with or
   without Morbidity. Morbidity adds little: it taxes Majesty but not Earth Meld, and the
   Sword's strike is unusable after an opposing Immortal Grapple.
