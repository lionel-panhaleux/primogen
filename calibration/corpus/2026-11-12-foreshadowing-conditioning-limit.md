## Question

If you use Foreshadowing Destruction and your prey is over ten, can you add a Conditioning ?

## Answer given

Yes, if the Foreshadowing Destruction is at superior. Played at [DOM] against a target with 10 or more pool, it doesn't count against the limit, so a Conditioning can still increase the bleed. At inferior it's a flat +1 bleed (limited), which does use the limit, and then Conditioning can't be added.

**What settles it** (official, from the rulings database):

> [DOM] Can be played against a target with 10 or more pool and does not count against the limit in this case: it will only have a lingering effect (+3 bleed if the target happens to have 9 or less). [PIB 20110817] [RTR 20191031]

Ruling: https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=100765#r-4BC3FFKE. The original post is by Pascal Bertrand, the sitting rules director, on 2011-08-17: https://usenet.krcg.org/t/vekn-8405/#8414

The card texts:
- **Foreshadowing Destruction:** "[dom] +1 bleed (limited). [DOM] +3 bleed if the target Methuselah has 9 or fewer pool (limited)."
- **Conditioning** (costs 1 blood): "[dom] +2 bleed (limited). [DOM] +3 bleed (limited)."

The advanced rules (curated, not verified by a rules director) say the same in general terms: "The limit counts an increase only while that increase is actually applying" (https://vtes-biased.github.io/vtes-advanced-rules/#3711-bleed-amount).

**Two points the evidence leaves open:**
- **The condition reads the bleed's target, not your prey as such.** The ruling turns on the target's pool. On an ordinary bleed of your prey the two are the same.
- **No ruling covers the case where Foreshadowing's +3 starts applying after Conditioning is played**, for example if the target drops to 9 or fewer during the action. No ruling names Foreshadowing Destruction and Conditioning together, so that case is unsettled. The rulings database has no ruling on Conditioning itself.

## Owner grade

Good rulings both. On foreshadowing destruction the end is slightly misleading: if the prey goes below 10 during action both modifiers apply - this has been discussed and confirmed in rulings threads. But maybe the source ruling is incomplete

## Correction

The ruling is right and so is the answer's yes; the closing "not settled" is wrong. If the
target drops to 9 or fewer pool during the action, both modifiers apply: the superior
Foreshadowing's +3 and the Conditioning's. Vincent Ripoll (Ankha), the sitting rules
director, said so in "About bleed modifiers": +6 bleed when the bleed is bounced back to a
target under 10 pool (2019-10-24, https://usenet.krcg.org/t/vekn-78044/#m3), and "Yes" to
both being active when a bleed with both played is bounced to a player under 10 pool
(2019-10-25, https://usenet.krcg.org/t/vekn-78044/#m13). The pack's own ruling already
pointed there ("a lingering effect (+3 bleed if the target happens to have 9 or less)"), as
did the advanced rules ("lingers, applying if the condition is later met"). The ruling cites
neither post: the source ruling is incomplete.
