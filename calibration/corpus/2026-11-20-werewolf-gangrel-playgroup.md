## Answer given

Welcome back from the dead! The deck isn't "actively bad": it has a clear theme, and Protean and Animalism run deep in the crypt. There is one legality problem and a few cards that work against each other. In order:

**1. The crypt is one card short.** It has 11 vampires and the minimum is 12. The groups are fine: groups 4 and 5 can be mixed, but group 6 can't go with group 4.

**2. Gangrel and Gangrel antitribu are different clans, and most of your library needs plain Gangrel.** These cards all require "Gangrel": Thing, Ecoterrorists, Zoo Hunting Ground, Backways, Renegade Garou and Double Deuce. So 3 of your 11 vampires (Janey Pickman, Céleste Lamontagne, Joseph Fischer) can't recruit your main werewolves, and Thing or Ecoterrorists can't add blood to them. The only thing they enable is Black Spiral Buddy, which needs an antitribu.
- **Fix:** go all-Gangrel. Drop the 3 antitribu and the 2 Buddies, and bring the crypt to 12 with group 4 and 5 Gangrel. Options: Crow (G5, cap 5, ANI for pro tha) and André the Manipulator (G5, cap 6, FOR PRO, +2 hand size while ready), plus a second Inyanga and a second Lillian.
- Inyanga is worth two copies here: *"Allies requiring a Gangrel cost Inyanga 1 fewer blood or pool."* With her, Renegade Garou costs 4 instead of 5.

**3. Walks-With-Might fights your own deck.** His text says *"Master cards cost you 1 additional pool to play"*, and you run 18 masters. The ruling confirms it only taxes his controller, meaning you ([LSJ](https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=102141#r-2NKICREQ)). Your 5 Tribute to the Master and 3 Blood Doll would all cost 1 pool each while he's out. Cut both copies.

**4. Sense the Savage Way needs a vampire of capacity 7 or more.** Only Art Morgan (×2) and Inyanga in your crypt qualify, so it's dead in most of your draws.

**5. Not enough reactions for a deck that wants to block.** You have 7 reactions. The established werewolf archetype ("Gangrel Renegades": 25 tournament-winning decks since 2021, the latest winning the Brazilian national championship this year with 4 Renegade Garou) runs about 25. Its main one fits your theme perfectly:
> **Eyes of the Wild**: Only usable by a locked Gangrel. This Gangrel unlocks and attempts to block. During this action, this Gangrel can burn 1 blood to get +1 intercept. Lock this Gangrel after action resolution if they did not block this action.

It shows up in 96% of winning decks that play Renegade Garou, alongside Thing (96%) and Ecoterrorists (96%). Your Thing, Ecoterrorists, Zoo and Tribute choices are exactly what those decks play, so you're on the right track.

**6. Eight Glancing Blow is a lot.** It reads *"Do not replace until your next unlock phase"*, so a hand holding two or three of them shrinks for a turn. Four is plenty.

**Suggested swap (library stays at 80):**
- **Crypt:** −Janey Pickman, −Céleste Lamontagne, −Joseph Fischer; +Crow, +André the Manipulator, +1 Inyanga, +1 Lillian (12 cards, all Gangrel).
- **Library cuts:** −2 Walks-With-Might, −2 Black Spiral Buddy, −2 Sense the Savage Way, −4 Glancing Blow.
- **Library adds:**
  - +8 Eyes of the Wild (defense).
  - +1 Cats' Guidance (defense).
  - +1 Rewilding, a Gangrel action: *"Burn a location and burn 2 pool from its controller."* It adds a little reach to the ousting plan, and 92% of the Renegade Garou winners play it.

**What to expect when playing it.** The deck's weak spot is ousting. Every werewolf has 0 bleed except Double Deuce (1, and +1 stealth). Jacob Fermor gives werewolves +1 bleed while he's ready, so with him out Double Deuce bleeds for 2 with stealth. That's your best bleeder, so protect Jacob. Otherwise your vampires bleed for 1. In practice the deck wins by surviving:
- Renegade Garou and Ossian enter combat with rival vampires as +1 stealth actions.
- Raven Spy, Cats' Guidance and Eyes of the Wild block.
- Art Morgan unlocks a werewolf each turn so it can act twice.
- Your prey grinds down slowly.

That's a fine, thematic playgroup deck. Games will just be long, and a fast bleed deck at the table is the matchup to watch. Decks with a lot of "Strike: combat ends" also blunt the werewolves. Two rulings worth knowing:
- Renegade Garou's and Buddy's additional strike is **mandatory** ([PIB](https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=101602#r-FEL744PZ)).
- Weighted Walking Stick can go on an ally, and it's at its best there.

## Correction

1. Bait and Switch is a classic: 95 of the 99 Gangrel Resistance decks of
   the TWDA since 2021-07-01 play it, 5.9 copies on average (vtes_card_stats,
   data of 2026-11-19). It requires a baron, and this crypt has none: the
   archetype's barons (Massimiliano, Casey Snyder, Kuyén) are G6. The answer
   should have offered it as the option for the bleed defense it names as
   the weak matchup, with its trade-off: barons move the crypt to G5/G6,
   which loses Jacob Fermor and Inyanga (G4).
2. Too verbose: a rule the list already satisfies (its crypt groups) and a
   ruling that only confirms the card's plain text (Walks-With-Might taxes
   his controller) need not be said.
