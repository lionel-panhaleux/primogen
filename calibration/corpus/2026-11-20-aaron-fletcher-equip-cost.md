## Question

When a minion uses the +1 stealth equip action to equip with one of the cards on Aaron Fletcher, do Cost and Requirement apply?

## Answer given

**Yes, as far as the evidence goes: requirements and cost apply. No ruling covers Aaron Fletcher yet**, since the card has been legal only since 2026-10-31.

**Why:** Aaron's text grants an equip action, not a free placement:
> Put up to three equipment cards from your hand and/or ash heap on Aaron, face down and out of play (…). Minions you control can equip with one of those cards (face up) as a +1 stealth equip action.

- **The rule:** the advanced rules (curated, not verified by a rules director) say *"Only 'put ... in play' bypasses requirements and cost … **Equip**, **recruit** and **employ** invoke the normal machinery, so requirements and cost apply as printed"* ([advanced 1.6.3](https://vtes-biased.github.io/vtes-advanced-rules/#163-cards-entering-play-abnormally)).
- **Aaron's card says "equip".** It never says "put in play".
- **Not the free equip from another minion.** The rulebook's equip from another minion you control is free ([advanced 3.7.3](https://vtes-biased.github.io/vtes-advanced-rules/#373-equip-actions), citing the rulebook). That case doesn't apply here: the cards on Aaron are *out of play*, not equipment he has, so taking one is like equipping from hand, which pays the cost.

**Consequences:**
- A clan or discipline equipment can only be equipped by a minion who meets its requirement.
- Its pool or blood cost is paid when the equip action is taken.
- It is still an equip action, so effects that modify equip actions reach it ([advanced 3.7.3.1](https://vtes-biased.github.io/vtes-advanced-rules/#3731-what-counts-as-an-equip-action)).

**What's ruled versus inferred:**
- **Ruled:** the closest official precedent, Concealed Weapon, equips from hand and its text prints *"(requirements and cost apply as normal)"* ([card text](https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=100392)).
- **Inferred:** applying the same to Aaron rests on the advanced rules' general principle. No rules director has spoken to Aaron himself. The archive has no post naming him (searched to 2026-11-14).
