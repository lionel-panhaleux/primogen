## Question

Anyone had any luck with Business Pressure and Awe in Toreador vote decks? In my current meta I
often see barons/princes even without much focus on politics, but when they get a Sabbat vote
as a cross-table / or just another vote-loaded deck, my game is set back by a mile, having zero
change to advance my political actions, unless I get lucky redirects/my predator is afk. So I'm
thinking about ramping up my vote strength. Currently I run Paragons and Bewitching Oration.
Already have few Conclaves and Versailles to help a bit.

## Answer given

Short version: against a vote-loaded cross-table, piling more votes onto your own side is the weaker answer. Cutting off their votes works better, and the Toreador vote winners already do that.

**Your two candidates, read from their text**

- **Business Pressure** ([card](https://static.krcg.org/card/businesspressure.jpg)): "Methuselahs can burn pool to get votes during the resolution of this card", +2 per pool (+3 at superior for you). Both sides can burn pool. Against another vote deck it starts a pool auction, and you pay first in the currency you're trying to oust them with. It also has to resolve on the spot: each Methuselah decides how much to burn "during the resolution of the effect. It does not give an ability for the rest of the political action" ([RTR 19960530](https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=100274#r-N66LT4HF)). Tournament winners agree: since 2021-07, 4 of 1494 TWDA decks run it, 4 of the 33 Endless Dance (Toreador vote) decks.
- **Awe** ([card](https://static.krcg.org/card/awe.jpg)): "+2X+1 votes" at superior, for X blood. It scales, but each extra vote costs blood, which you'd rather spend on more actions. It's a real option: 15 of the 33 Endless Dance winners run it, usually at 2 copies.

**What you're already running**

- If "Paragons" means **Paragon** (the master), it is Ventrue only: "Put this card on a ready Ventrue…". A Toreador can't use it. I assume you mean **Perfect Paragon** (+3 votes for 1 blood, plus −1 intercept for younger vampires and allies at superior). That's the vote modifier these decks run most: 29 of the 33 Endless Dance winners.
- **Bewitching Oration** (+4 at superior, free) is a fine partner. Both are already the efficient way to add votes.

**What actually beats a Sabbat vote deck: removing its votes**

- **Closed Session** ([card](https://static.krcg.org/card/closedsession.jpg)): "Requires a prince, justicar or Inner Circle member… before votes and ballots are cast. Non-Camarilla vampires cannot cast votes or ballots during this referendum." Against a Sabbat cross-table, this wipes out exactly the votes that hurt you, for zero blood. The usual crypt of these decks meets the requirement: Catalina Vega and Flávio Gonçalves, the vampires the Endless Dance winners run most, are both Camarilla princes. Five of the 33 Endless Dance winners run it.
- **Camarilla Conclave** ([card](https://static.krcg.org/card/camarillaconclave.jpg)), which you already have: "give each non-Camarilla vampire -1 vote" per counter, 4 counters. It needs a justicar or Inner Circle member, though, and a vampire can drop below zero but "net negative votes do not subtract from the total" ([ANK 20251125](https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=102269#r-OEP2Q3RM)). Against a single big titled voter it chips away. It doesn't erase them.
- **Scalpel Tongue** (cel+pre): "Choose a vampire who has cast votes or ballots in this referendum. The chosen vampire is locked and abstains." Toreador have both disciplines. It answers their biggest voter after they commit. This is the standard tool: 24 of the 33 Endless Dance winners run it, usually 2 copies.
- **Telepathic Vote Counting** (aus) cancels the referendum or forces an abstain at superior. It's more useful against their votes than for passing yours.
- **Elysium: The Palace of Versailles** gives each of your titled Camarilla vampires +1 vote, so it grows with your princes. It's fine as you have it.

**Suggested change**

Keep Perfect Paragon and Bewitching Oration as your vote adders. For the slots you were considering for Business Pressure or Awe, take 2 Scalpel Tongue and 1–2 Closed Session. Those cards turn "they outvote me" into "they can't vote". Awe is reasonable as a third adder if your blood is comfortable. I'd skip Business Pressure: against a vote deck it hands them a matching lever.
