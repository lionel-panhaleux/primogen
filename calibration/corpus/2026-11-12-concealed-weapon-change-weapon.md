## Question

I played Concealed Weapon for a Magnum I had in hand but drew an Ivory Bow to replace, can I change my mind and play the Ivory Bow as the concealed weapon ?

## Answer given

No, for two independent reasons. Concealed Weapon stays committed to the .44 Magnum you named.

1. **The weapon is fixed when Concealed Weapon is played.** You name it then, from your hand, before you draw Concealed Weapon's replacement. The Ivory Bow came in as that replacement, so it was never a legal choice. The ruling (official, from the rulings database): "The equipment or retainer must be announced by name, and in hand, when the card or effect is declared (before the card, if any, is replaced), but need not to be shown until the effect resolves. [LSJ 20100130] [ANK 20180925-2] [LSJ 19991207]", https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=100392#r-AE7KZCMM.
   - Vincent Ripoll (Ankha), the sitting rules director, on 2018-09-25: "You just have to say: 'I play Concealed Weapon on a .44 Magnum'" (https://usenet.krcg.org/t/vekn-77029/#90757).
   - L. Scott Johnson, the sitting rules director, on 1999-12-07: "The weapon to be equipped must be named/selected when the card is played" (https://usenet.krcg.org/t/aQaOTYwC-fg/#m3).
2. **The Ivory Bow can't be concealed anyway.** Concealed Weapon: "This minion equips with a non-unique weapon card from your hand (requirements and cost apply as normal). The weapon cannot cost 3 or more pool or inflict (with a regular strike) aggravated damage or 4 or more damage." Ivory Bow: "Unique weapon. Strike: 1R aggravated damage." It fails both on being unique and on its aggravated damage.

**At the table:** Concealed Weapon resolves with the Magnum as you announced it. The Magnum is eligible: ".44 Magnum: Cost: 2 pool. Weapon: gun. Strike: 2R damage, with 1 optional maneuver each combat." You pay its 2 pool, and the Ivory Bow stays in your hand.

The advanced rules (curated, not verified by a rules director) say the same in general terms: "A card named from hand must be in hand when the naming card is played, before the replacement for that card is drawn. It need not be shown: you may set it apart face down, e.g. {Disguised Weapon}" (https://vtes-biased.github.io/vtes-advanced-rules/#1142-cards-named-from-hand).
