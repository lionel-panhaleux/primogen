## Question

Can you play Quickness sup first, then Blur ?

## Answer given

Yes. Superior Quickness is "Additional strike that does not count against the limit", so it doesn't use the one limited additional-strike effect per round. Blur can still be played. The catch is the timing: you make the Quickness strike first, then play Blur.

The sequence:
1. After the normal pair of strikes, play Quickness at superior and make its additional strike.
2. Then play Blur. That's the round's one limited effect: one more strike at inferior, two at superior.

You can't play both and stack three strikes at once. A further additional strike can only be gained once those already granted have been made.

**The evidence:**
- **Quickness ruling** (official): "[CEL] A limited additional strike can be played before or after. [LSJ 20001206] [ANK 20211124]", https://rulings.krcg.org/index.html?uid=101532#r-OANZ2AOH.
  - Vincent Ripoll (Ankha), the sitting rules director, confirmed it with "Correct" on 2021-11-24 (https://usenet.krcg.org/t/vekn-79501/#103982). The question was whether superior Quickness can be played after Blur or Dust Up, and the post he confirmed said "Yes, you can play it before or after if played at superior, the order doesn't matter."
- **The timing** comes from L. Scott Johnson, the sitting rules director, on 2000-12-06 (https://usenet.krcg.org/t/kFIO74LxqFQ/#m4): "Resolve the pending additional strikes before gaining any further additional strikes (note that this is only applicable to superior Quickness and Jacko…)".
- **The rulebook** (official, https://archon.vekn.net/help/rules#advanced-rules-additional-strikes) says: "Additional strikes are announced and performed only after the first pair of strikes is completed… A minion cannot use more than one card or effect to gain additional strikes per round of combat. This rule is reminded on those cards by the '(limited)' card text."
- **The advanced rules** (curated, not verified by a rules director, https://vtes-biased.github.io/vtes-advanced-rules/#435-additional-strikes) say: "a further additional strike is gained only once those already granted have been made, e.g. {Quickness} [CEL] after {Blur} [CEL]'s two."

**Two related points:**
- Quickness also says "A vampire can play only one Quickness each round."
- At inferior, Quickness is "Additional strike (limited)", so it takes the limit and Blur can't follow in that round.
