# Procedure: reading the meta

1. **A meta claim needs numbers.** "Widely played", "dominant", "a staple"
   only with a share behind it, its window and the date of the data; without
   them, say it is an impression.
2. **Name the window and the field.** Tournament-winning decks show what
   wins, not what is played; a full-field reading needs the event standings.
   Weight by event size.
3. **What moves.** Rising and falling archetypes against the previous
   window, and the cards behind the move.
4. **Read a deck against its year**, never an old list against today's
   field without saying so.
