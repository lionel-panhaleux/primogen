From the owner-graded entry `calibration/corpus/2026-11-20-top-tier-decks.md`,
graded clean ("very good"). Answered 2026-11-20 on the data of 2026-11-19
(TWDA to 2026-10-25). Re-checked by every regression re-run; the shares age
with the TWDA.

What it shows: the window and its size named, shares weighted by players,
the longer window beside the shorter one so a trend reads as a trend, each
archetype carried by its core cards rather than a name alone, a small
sample flagged, and the limits closing the answer: a flat meta, the share
in no archetype, wins and not play.

The question: "What are the top tier decks nowadays?"

---

These are winning shares over the 12 months to 2026-10-25 (358 decks, weighted by players), with the last 6 months in brackets.

1. **Gangrel Resistance**: 11.1% (12.9%). The clear leader, and rising. Its core is Casey Snyder, Kamile Paukstys, Indira and Kuyén, with Form of the Bat, Deep Ecology, Ecoterrorists, Bait and Switch, Earth Meld and Carfax Abbey.
2. **Pop Captivation**: 6.1% (4.1%, falling). Aaradhya, Üresség, Khin Aye and Věnceslava, with Banishment, Cold War, Deflection, Unthinkable Humiliation and Voter Captivation.
3. **The Unnamed**: 5.0% (7.0%, rising). The unnamed and Anarch Convert, with Unleash Hell's Fury, Enkil Cog, Instantaneous Transformation and Form of Mist.
4. **Lasombra Pop**: 4.5% (5.7%). Gratiano, Ambrosio and Francisco Domingo, with Banishment, Deflection, Shadow Play and Elysian Fields. It is only 6 decks, so the share rests on a few large events.
5. **Ravnos Bonds**: 4.5% (4.8%). Gathii, Phaibun and Roberto Rivamonte, with Lost in Crowds, Visions of Gehenna, Break the Bonds and Elder Impersonation.

Next come Living Museum (3.5%) and Juliet's Dream (2.7%), then a long tail where nothing else reaches 2.5%. Over the last 6 months, Nephandus (2.8%) and Qawiyya Caine (2.2%) are the new arrivals near the top.

Read with care: the meta is flat. Only Gangrel Resistance passes 10%, and 10.7% of winning decks fit no archetype. These are shares of wins, not of what is played. Primogen has no full-field data.
