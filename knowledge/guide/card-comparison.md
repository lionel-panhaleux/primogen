# Procedure: comparing or evaluating cards

"Better" is per role, never absolute: Minion Tap beats Villein in a crypt of
big capacities (Villein moves at most 5), Villein wins on mid capacities.

1. Ground both cards: text, requirements, rulings.
2. Define the role and the deck, or name the two or three realistic
   contexts.
3. Who plays it: play rates, the cards it is played with, the archetypes
   that carry it, from primogen's numbers.
4. Answer per context, with the numbers, and say which context dominates in
   practice.
